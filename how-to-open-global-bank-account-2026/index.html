How to Open a Global Bank Account in 2026

Bank accounts without residency. For digital nomads, crypto users, and international business owners who have outgrown the need for permission.

March 2026 8 min read Global
Why It Matters

Why You Need a Global Bank Account in 2026

Traditional banking ties accounts to residency, credit history, and physical presence. For the globally mobile, this creates real barriers.

The Problem with Traditional Banks

Most require proof of residency, local ID, and in-person visits. They block crypto-related transactions, charge excessive fees for international wires, and can freeze accounts based on algorithms — not actual problems.

A global bank account gives you:

  • Multi-currency support without conversion fees
  • International wire transfers at a fraction of traditional bank rates
  • No residency requirement to open or maintain the account
  • Business banking features without physical branches
  • Crypto-friendly policies that do not freeze your account when you use a DEX
Step by Step

How to Open Your Global Account

The process is simpler than you think — often under 24 hours from start to funded.

  1. Choose the Right Platform Not all global banks are equal. Look for: no residency requirement, crypto-friendly policy, multi-currency accounts, and a global VISA card. Trustyfy checks all four.
  2. Prepare Your Documents Most global banks need: valid passport, proof of address (utility bill or bank statement from your home country), and for business accounts — company registration documents.
  3. Complete the Online Application Apply through the platform's website. Most approvals come within 1-5 business days. Some platforms offer same-day approval for basic accounts.
  4. Fund Your Account Once approved, fund via wire transfer, crypto deposit (USDC, ETH, etc.), or international transfer. Choose a platform that accepts crypto deposits if you need to fund from exchange wallets or DeFi protocols.

What to Look for in a Global Bank in 2026

The best global banks for crypto users and international professionals offer:

  • Self-custody wallets — You control your keys, not the bank
  • No account freezes — Clear policies on crypto transactions
  • Multi-chain support — Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, and more
  • Global VISA — Spend anywhere in the world
  • Super Swap — Convert crypto to fiat at fair rates
